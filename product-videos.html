chapter 01product marketing

the thinking✦

The brief: I talk to a lot of our users. Most of it is feedback, and it goes back to the product team and into our content. On those calls, users brought up a lot of problems, and many of them our features were already solving: the AI voice tutor, mentor calls, mock tests, the study planner, the quiz builder, RC Verse.

The result: The solution was right there on the platform. People just didn't know it existed. A launch video is the industry standard, and we made one, but every feature also needed its own video, simple enough to get in a few seconds.
