01

The thinking

→ where it started

I talk to a lot of our users. Most of it is feedback: what's working, what isn't. That goes back to the product team, and it shapes our content too.

On those calls, users brought up a lot of problems. Some we could go and solve. But many of them, our features were already solving: the AI voice tutor, mentor calls, mock tests, the study planner, the quiz builder, RC Verse. The solution was right there on the platform. People just didn't know it existed.

A launch video is the industry standard, and we made one. But one video wasn't enough. Every feature needed its own, and it had to be simple enough to get in a few seconds.

“The solution was already there. People just didn't know it existed.”

02

The strategy

→ the plan

  1. Talk to usersFeedback goes into the product, and into the content.
  2. Spot the gapProblems users raised that a feature already solved, if only they knew about it.
  3. One launch filmThe industry standard: the whole product, in one go.
  4. One clip per featureEvery feature gets its own video, simple enough to get in seconds.

03

The design

→ how it looks

Big type on Optima blue, cut with real product screens. The feature clips are short and silent, made to loop. The launch film was made in Kite, and the clips with Claude, vibe coding all the way.
