→ who lumera is

Lumera is a contemporary jewellery brand. The goal was a minimal, elegant visual language that still holds its own on a busy feed.

Clean layouts, soft luxury, and the same look everywhere it shows up. Set in Atkinson Hyperlegible and Bricolage Grotesque.
