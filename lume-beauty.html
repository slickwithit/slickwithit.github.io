01

The brand

→ who lumé is

Lumé is a modern makeup brand about bold self-expression, individuality and unapologetic confidence. It's not about covering up. It's about turning up the features you already have, and having fun with it.

Personality

Bold, creative, unapologetic

Confident and self-expressive. It never asks for permission.

Visual language

The feature is the hero

High-contrast close-ups of eyes and lips, minimal backgrounds, strong type.

“Quietly bold.”

The work

→ the posters: one feature, front and centre

A feature-led series about precision, confidence and expressive beauty. Tap one to see it big.

→ and the reel
