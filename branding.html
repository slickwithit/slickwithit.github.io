01

The thinking

→ what optima stands for

There's too much content online, and not everyone learns the same way. Optima gives you personalised prep for MBA entrances, starting with CAT: AI that adapts to you, guided by real human mentors. "Clarity over chaos" is that promise in three words, so everything we make carries it.

“We need our identity. It's important to be seen.”

02

The strategy

→ start minimal, then build on it

  1. Minimal firstPlayful, but never messy. It delivers the message and gets through.
  2. Reward the ones who chose usEvery paid user got a kit: t-shirt, tote, diary and keychain.
  3. Be seen offlineStandees and brochures for the events we go to, so it's clear what we are.
  4. Say it by handEach kit came with a handwritten, personalised message card.
