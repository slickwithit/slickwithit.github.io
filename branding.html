optima learn · chapter 01project 01

the system✦

The brief: Optima Learn is a new name in a crowded CAT prep category, where most brands compete on volume and urgency. Its audience is students who are overwhelmed and sceptical of big promises.

The result: One rule set that every touchpoint follows: one promise, one blue, calm copy and a mascot for warmth. The brand reads as one thing on a phone screen, a standee or a keychain.

chapter 1

the promise

Students aren't short on content. They're short on direction.the insight

Optima Learn is personalised CAT prep: an AI coach that adapts to each student, guided by 1:1 mentors who have already cracked the exam. The category sounds loud, cluttered and fear-led, so the promise claims the opposite space in three words.

the colour · one blue

Holding the palette this tight is a deliberate trade: less variety, much faster recognition.

the voice · never fear

Prep isn't hard because you're not smart. It's hard because no one tells you what to do next.brochure

optima learn · chapter 02project 01

the welcome kit✦

The brief: Early users took a chance on a new brand. Reward that decision, build loyalty from day one, and get Optima seen outside the app.

The result: Every paid user received a kit: a tee, a tote, a diary, a keychain and a handwritten note from the founder. The brand now travels into colleges, offices and coaching centres, carried by the people who chose it.

chapter 2

optima learn · chapter 03project 01

print for events✦

The brief: At offline events, people give a stand a few seconds as they walk past. The print had to work at walking pace.

The result: Every piece follows the same order: the promise first, proof second, the product third, and a QR code to sign up on the spot. Same blue, mascot and tone as the app, so print and digital feel like one brand.

the mascot✦

The brief: A system this clean can feel cold. The brand needed warmth without adding noise.

The result: A mascot with 20 moods, one for every moment a student goes through, from a tough mock to a final result. It reacts, celebrates and keeps students company across the app, social and print.

dough it · chapter 01project 02

the brand✦

The brief: Brief Me Baby brief #80: Dough It, a ready-to-bake cookie dough brand. Indulgent flavours, gooey centres and the satisfaction of homemade cookies without the mess, for late-night cravings and spontaneous sweet treats.

The result: A brand that sounds like its product: playful, gooey, cheeky, homemade and made for late nights. A hand-lettered wordmark, a rolling-pin mascot and a tagline that says exactly what it is: ready-to-bake cookie dough.

chapter 1

dough it · chapter 02project 02

the system✦

The brief: A dessert brand has to look edible. The palette, type and pattern had to feel warm and indulgent, and still hold together across tubs, boxes, bags and social.

The result: Four colours taken from the product itself: milk blue, chocopie, raw dough and butter. Milk blue and chocopie lead every layout; raw dough and butter warm things up as backgrounds, flavour colours and highlights. No other colours, and every typeface has one job.

chapter 2

dough it · chapter 03project 02

the packaging✦

The brief: Four flavours that would sit on one shelf and still read as one family, across tubs, a box of 12 and delivery bags.

The result: One tub, four flavours: classic choc chip, double choc fudge, salted caramel and brown butter vanilla. The chocolate drip changes with every flavour while the wordmark and mascot stay put, so each tub is easy to spot and the family stays clear.

chapter 3

dough it · chapter 04project 02

the carousel✦

The brief: Present the identity as one Instagram carousel, so people meet the brand the way a customer would: one piece at a time.

The result: A 10-slide carousel that walks from the wordmark to the mascot, palette, flavours and packaging, and ends on the moment the whole brand is built for: it's 11pm. you know what to do.
